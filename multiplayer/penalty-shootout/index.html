How it works

  1. Shooter answers a question. Right = the kick is on target. Wrong = it flies over the bar.
  2. The shooter secretly picks a corner: left, middle or right.
  3. Pass the device. The keeper answers a question. Right = the keeper can dive. Wrong = the keeper slips.
  4. The keeper picks a corner. Same corner as the shot = saved! Players swap roles every kick.
