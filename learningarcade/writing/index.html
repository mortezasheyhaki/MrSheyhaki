Before You Submit

Step 01 · Structure

Full Sentence Order

Check subject + verb + object order and auxiliary placement (do / does / is / are) in questions.

Step 02 · Agreement

He / She / It Verbs

Watch for third-person -s endings in daily routines and pronoun shifts when writing about someone else.

Step 03 · Polish

Capitals & Punctuation

Start every sentence with a capital letter and finish cleanly with a period or question mark.
