Complete the sentences

Use the superlative form of the adjective in brackets.

Example: Maria bought the most expensive (expensive) phone in the shop.
1Daniel's the(bad)player in the team.
2Is Russia the(big)country in the world?
3My neighbour's grandfather is the(old)person I know.
4My sister's the(funny)person in my class.
5Fog's the(dangerous)weather for drivers.
6We all felt nervous but Marco was the(nervous).
7Is Copacabana the(good)beach in Brazil?
