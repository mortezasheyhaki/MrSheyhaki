Role-play

Practice with a partner. One person is Sue, the other is Amy.

Sue

  1. Hello.
  2. I'm fine, thank you. And you?
  3. What's your name?
  4. My name's Sue. Nice to meet you!
  5. Good bye.

Amy

  1. Hi. How are you?
  2. Fine, thanks.
  3. My name's Amy. What's your name?
  4. Nice to meet you, too.
  5. Bye. Have a nice time.
